📝 About the exam

110 marks
out of 160 for the whole course — about 69% of your grade
2 h 30 min
one question paper, sat in the exam diet in April or May
Section 1
20 marks of shorter questions
Section 2
90 marks of longer, structured questions
  • Answer all the questions. Any part of the course specification can be tested.
  • You are given the Higher Engineering Science data booklet. Know where everything is in it before the exam.
  • The front cover reminds you to show all working and units, and explains how many significant figures to give. See Top Tips.

Source: Qualifications Scotland Higher Engineering Science question papers, 2022–2026.

🎯 Advice from the course reports

Don’t round too early
  • Keep the full calculator value until the end, and round only the final answer. Every report from 2024 to 2026 says that rounding in the middle of a calculation cost marks.
  • Use the π button, not 3.14. Top Tips: when to round
Check your units
  • Wrong units led to wrong final answers. Convert mm to m, kN to N and so on before substituting. Top Tips: units
Write full descriptions Most demanding
  • Written answers are the weakest area every year. Use the labels on the diagram, follow the chain from input to output, quote the values and link your answer to the context. Top Tips: describe and explain
  • Pneumatics: 3/2 valves actuate, 5/2 valves change state, cylinders outstroke or instroke. Say when a valve unactuates and cuts off a signal, and explain the job of any valve that prevents signal conflict. Pneumatics notes
  • Roles of engineers: two different tasks, each specific to the context and to the phase named in the question. Roles and impacts notes
Work methodically through circuit calculations
  • Analogue electronics is found harder than digital electronics or structures. Set out each step: for a MOSFET that is not fully on, find the voltage across the load first, then IDS, then read VGS from the table or graph. The MOSFET
  • For a transistor, the power dissipated is VCE × IC, where VCE = Vsupply − the voltage across the load.
Cover the whole course
  • The paper samples the whole course specification, so revise every topic. Past papers and marking instructions (below) show the level of detail needed.

Source: Qualifications Scotland Higher Engineering Science course reports, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
