📝 About the exam

110 marks
out of 160 for the whole course — about 69% of your grade
1 h 50 min
one question paper, sat in the exam diet in April or May
Section 1
20 marks of shorter questions
Section 2
90 marks of longer, structured questions
  • Answer all the questions. Any part of the course specification can be tested.
  • You are given the National 4/5 Engineering Science data booklet. Use its formulae and methods.
  • Show your working and give units. See Top Tips for significant figures, units and prefixes.

Source: Qualifications Scotland National 5 Engineering Science course specification and question papers.

🎯 Advice from the course reports

“Explain” questions Most demanding
  • Give one cause that fits the context and the effect it leads to. Two causes, or a cause with an unrelated effect, will not get full marks. Top Tips: describe and explain
Pneumatics Most demanding
  • Use the correct terms: 3/2 valves actuate, 5/2 valves change state, and pistons outstroke or instroke. This has been in every recent report. Pneumatics notes
  • Learn the names of the symbols, including main air, exhaust air, pilot air and the diaphragm actuator.
Units for pressure and stress
  • The expected unit is Nmm−2. Don’t convert the area to m2 unless the question asks for it. If you do give the answer in N m−2 or Pa, you must convert the area to m2 first.
  • Work out the piston area first. Don’t put the diameter straight into the pressure formula.
Describing systems
  • Say whether the system is open or closed loop.
  • For a closed-loop system, say that the sensor measures the actual output and feeds it back, that the control sub-system compares the set level with the actual level, and what happens to the output when there is an error and when there is none.
Analogue circuits
  • Answer the exact thing the question asks about, such as the change in the LDR’s resistance or the change in Vin, and follow the chain from input to output.
Roles of engineers
  • Give two different problem-solving tasks, each linked to the context. Avoid general statements such as “design the structure” or “develop the circuit”. Roles and disciplines notes
Emerging technologies
  • An emerging technology must be new and not yet in commercial use. Artificial intelligence, electric vehicles and self-driving cars have not earned full marks.

Source: Qualifications Scotland National 5 Engineering Science course reports, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
